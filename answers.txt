Pros of Agile Methodology:
- It is flexible and there is not much rigidity
- Frequent feedback and checks keeps team aligned

Cons of Agile Methodology:
- It is less predictable in terms of timelines since project can be prone to change
- It is hard to scale for bigger teams


Scrum Mater: Handles all scrum related tasks including JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings etc. Focuses more on the people in the team

Product Owner: Helps set direction for the team, handles releases and stakeholder management. Focuses more on the product
