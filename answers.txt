Pros of Agile Methodology:
- It is flexible and there is not much rigidity.
- Frequent feedback and checks keeps team aligned.

Cons of Agile Methodology:
- It is less predictable in terms of timelines since project can be prone to change.
- It is hard to scale for bigger teams.




Scrum Master: Handles all scrum related tasks including JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings etc. Focuses more on the people in the team.

Product Owner: Helps set direction for the team, handles releases and stakeholder management. Focuses more on the product.




Sprint Planning Meeting:
- Determine what to finsih in the coming sprint.
- Move stories from backlogs to current sprint and start the sprint.

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
- Short 10 minute meeting that happens every day of the sprint.
- Team members discuss what they worked on and what they plan to finish that day.

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
- Occurs at the end of the sprint.
- Discuss what was completed and move incomplete work to the backlog.




Epic: It represents high level or bigger pieces of work for the project.

Story: A user story is the smallest unit of work that needs to be done.

Bug: A bug is a problem which impairs the functions of a product.

Task: A task represents work that may not offer value to the final procuts directly such as documentation.
